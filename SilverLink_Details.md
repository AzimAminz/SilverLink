## Slide 3: 1. The Problem

* **The Issue:** As townships mature, communities face a rapidly aging population with limited daytime support.
* **Who is affected:** Elderly residents are left alone at home for extended hours, while their young, working adult children experience high stress and anxiety regarding their parents' safety.
* **Why it is important:** Traditional property management handles infrastructure but cannot provide ad-hoc, personalized human care (e.g., checking in after a fall, delivering a quick meal), leading to critical gaps in community wellbeing and safety.

\---

## Slide 4: 2. Target User

* **Primary Beneficiary (The Care Receiver): Elderly residents who need occasional, non-medical assistance or wellness checks.**
* **Secondary Beneficiary (The Requester): Elderly residents who request help themselves and working adults who arrange assistance for their parents.**
* **Tertiary Beneficiary (The Fulfiller): Verified residents (freelancers, work-from-home professionals, stay-at-home parents) who help their neighbours while earning Community Points and recognition.**
* \---

## Slide 5: 3. Your Solution

* **Proposed Solution:** "SilverLink" – A decentralized, hyper-local micro-tasking network built exclusively for Sime Darby Property townships.

**How it Works \& Helps:**

* SilverLink connects residents needing short-term assistance with verified neighbours.
* Helpers earn Community Points based on task difficulty after completing tasks.
* These points determine their leaderboard rankings and unlock achievement badges, encouraging active community participation.



\---

## Slide 6: 4. Innovation

* **Unique Value Proposition:** SilverLink shifts the paradigm from centralized, top-down property management to a decentralized, community-driven care model.
* **Differentiation:** Unlike standard gig-economy apps (like Grab or TaskRabbit) that rely on external strangers, SilverLink uses a closed-loop, verified network of actual neighbors, fostering a high-trust environment tailored specifically for township safety and social cohesion.

\---

## Slide 7: 5. How it Works

* **Theme Focus:** PEOPLE (Community Wellbeing \& Safety).
* **Technical Workflow:**

  * **Request: A family member or authorised user posts a non-medical assistance request for an elderly resident.**
  * **Broadcast: The backend notifies eligible, verified residents within the participating township.**
  * **Action: A neighbour accepts the task, completes the assistance, and submits completion confirmation.**
  * **Reward: SilverLink awards Community Points based on the predefined task category after the task is verified.**
  * **Ranking: The system updates the helper's total points and monthly leaderboard position, recognising residents who make meaningful community contributions.**

\---

## Slide 8: 6. Impact

* **Positive Outcomes:**

  * **For the Elderly:** Immediate assistance and reduced isolation.
  * **For Families:** Peace of mind knowing a verified neighbor is just minutes away.
  * **For the Community:** Encourages participation through Community Points, leaderboards, and achievement badges, recognising helpful residents and strengthening community bonds.
  * **For Management:** Increases maintenance fee collection rates through the community point offset incentive.

## Slide 9: 7. Feasibility

* **Realistic Implementation:** Highly feasible. The platform relies on a proven, scalable tech stack: a cross-platform mobile frontend (Flutter) and a secure relational backend (Laravel, MySQL).
* **Resources \& Deployment:** Can be developed rapidly and deployed as a closed pilot program in a single, mature phase of a Sime Darby Property township to test user adoption and refine the community point economy before wider rollout.

\---

## Slide 10: 7. Implementation

* **Phase 1: Development \& Rigorous Testing:** Build the mobile interfaces and REST APIs. Crucially, implement comprehensive interface and performance testing to ensure the system flawlessly handles concurrent task acceptances and secure database transactions.
* **Phase 2: Security \& Onboarding:** Integrate management office data to strictly verify resident identities.
* **Phase 3: Pilot Launch:** Roll out to a targeted neighborhood, closely monitoring the speed of task fulfillment and user feedback to optimize the notification algorithms.
